# Data licence

`public/jmdict-en.json.gz` is a compact extract of the **JMdict** Japanese–English dictionary:
written forms, readings, English meanings and usage tags. It was made with `build_dictionary.py`.

This publication has included material from the JMdict dictionary files in accordance with the
licence provisions of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

- CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The extract is shared under the same licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The app also queries these services live, from the user's browser; no data from them is stored in
this repository:

- Wikipedia (text CC BY-SA) and Wikidata (CC0)
- NDL Subject Headings, obtained from Web NDL Authorities (National Diet Library, Japan)
- CiNii Research (National Institute of Informatics, Japan), with each user's own CiNii app ID
